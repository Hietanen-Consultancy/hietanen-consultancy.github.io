Rules switched off in `.htmlvalidate.json` and why:

- `void-style`, `attr-case`, `attribute-boolean-style` — React's SSR output
  writes `<meta charSet="utf-8"/>` and `async=""`; valid HTML5, different spelling.
- `valid-id` — TanStack Start injects a `$tsr-stream-barrier` marker element.
- `long-title`, `no-inline-style`, `require-sri` — deliberate: SEO titles, Tailwind
  arbitrary values, Google Fonts without SRI hashes.
