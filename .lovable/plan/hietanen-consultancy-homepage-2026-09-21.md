## Goal
Create a polished one-page consultancy site based on Tuomas Hietanen’s verified career experience, not generic consulting claims, supplemented by verified public work from the Thorium GitHub and Bitbucket portfolios.

## Page content
- Lead with technology leadership for fintech, AI, cloud, and regulated platforms.
- Present focused services around CTO/advisory, regulated fintech and FX platforms, AI agent engineering, cloud/software architecture, and functional .NET/F# systems.
- Highlight relevant public work such as maintained open-source libraries, quantum computing, LLM tooling, and multi-agent development where repository evidence supports it.
- Establish credibility with selected facts from the CV: 25+ years in software, CTO/co-founder experience, $200M+ capital raised at Finular, 800K+ registered users, and 99.99% platform uptime.
- Add a concise career timeline covering Hietanen Consultancy, Finular, Basware, Tieto-Tapiola, and earlier consulting work.
- End with a direct contact invitation without inventing an email address or phone number.

## Visual direction
Use the “Diagonal glass” concept: crisp Nordic typography, cool neutral surfaces, bright cyan accents, restrained glass details, and calm motion. Replace generic office imagery with a generated architectural technology image that supports the specialist positioning.

## Technical details
- Implement at `/` from a mobile-first layout, then progressively adapt it for tablet and desktop.
- Define all colors and typography as semantic design tokens.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata.
- Keep navigation links and contact actions functional.
