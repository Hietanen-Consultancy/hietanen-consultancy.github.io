The problem

Enterprises rent their AI, and cannot maintain the AI they build.

Rented models, rented terms

A hosted AI subscription sends customer records, contracts and code to a third party, bills every request, and can change or retire the model at any time. Compliance teams in banks, insurers and public bodies say no to the most valuable uses.

Built by specialists, not by the engineering team

In-house model work is done by a data scientist in research tools the company's own software engineers do not use. It ships as a one-off; when that person leaves, nobody can maintain it. The business systems it should serve run on a different platform entirely.

Regulation is arriving

The EU AI Act, DORA and data-residency rules ask who trained the model, on what, and who controls it. A model the company trained itself, on data it can trace, is the simplest answer to all three.
