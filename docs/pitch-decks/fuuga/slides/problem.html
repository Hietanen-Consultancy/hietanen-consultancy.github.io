The problem

Enterprises rent their AI, and cannot maintain the AI they build.

Rented models, no control

Data leaves the company, every request is billed, and the vendor can change or retire the model under a live product.

In-house models today are a specialist's side project

A data scientist builds them in Python tools the engineering team does not use. When that person leaves, nobody can maintain them.

Regulation asks who owns the model

For a high-risk use such as credit scoring, the EU AI Act requires a technical file describing the training data. DORA requires an exit plan for each critical ICT supplier.
