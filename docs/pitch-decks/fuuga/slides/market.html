About 900 European banks and insurers run on .NET and cannot put their data into a hosted model. At £60k a year each, that is a £55m-a-year market; thirty-five customers in year three is 4% of it. Detail — 9,163 regulated banks and insurers: 4,834 EU credit institutions (ECB, end 2024), 3,037 EEA insurers (EIOPA, February 2024), and 708 deposit-takers and 584 insurers regulated by the UK PRA. — About 30% run their core systems on .NET (29.9% of professional developers use C# extensively, Stack Overflow 2025): about 2,750. — About one in three cannot use a hosted model at all: air-gapped estates, sovereignty rules, or a supervisor who wants training evidence: about 916. — At the projections' mix, with managed operation at £100k, the average is £80k and the market about £73m. — The registers are fact; the two shares are estimates, tested by screening the list. Answers — Why .NET? The core systems already run on it, staffed by engineers the bank already employs. — Doesn't Azure OpenAI in-region answer residency? For most buyers. Not for air-gapped estates, software shipped into others' networks, or a regulator who wants evidence; that is the market. — Who else buys? Implementation partners, Nordic and smaller-language organisations, and vendors shipping on-premises .NET products.
