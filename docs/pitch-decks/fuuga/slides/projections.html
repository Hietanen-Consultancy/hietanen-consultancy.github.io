Hietanen Consultancy · Fuuga · 18 · Illustrative scenario; Year 1 = the twelve months after close; drivers on the next slide

A scenario built from the drivers on the next slide, not a forecast with evidence behind it yet. Where the profit is — Consulting earns about 45% gross margin, running the platform about 60%, continuity and partner revenue about 80%. — Consulting and managed operation together are 87%, 72% and 58% of gross profit in years one to three. Support and partners grow underneath, but the people who build and run the engines are the business. — OEM licences and per-token hosting are long-term doors, not in these numbers. How the lines are built — Recurring lines count customers signed evenly through the year: revenue is the average of the opening and closing count times the price. — Paying organisations at year end: four, twelve and thirty-five, assumed half run by us and half running it themselves. — Managed operation at £100k: two, six and seventeen organisations — £0.10m, £0.40m and £1.15m. — Continuity at £60k: two, six and eighteen organisations — £0.06m, £0.24m and £0.72m. — Partners at £30k: five in year two and twenty in year three — £0.08m and £0.38m. — Thirty-five organisations is about 4% of the roughly 900 on the market slide. — Headcount at year end: 8, 12 and 18.
