Hietanen Consultancy · Fuuga · 08

A public showcase: the pipeline works end to end, and every result is measured. Retraining the open decision model Laya took accuracy on unseen emotion cases from 59% to 90%, far ahead of the hosted, closed Jev at 58.5%, and none of six public benchmarks fell on the retrained text model. Detail — Laya: 58.7% as published, 89.9% after 6.5 minutes on 4,000 rows on one RTX 4070, exported back in its own format. — Jev, the same 2,000 questions through its API: 58.5%. Retrained Laya is right on 684 of the 741 cases they decide differently (p < 0.0001). — Without a person, when at least 90% sure: as published, it decided 68% of cases alone and was right on 66% of those; retrained, it decides 92% alone and is right on 94%. — Qwen3-1.7B, 80 MB of private text, 2 h 18 min: five benchmarks held or rose; TruthfulQA rose eight points. Answers — Was Jev treated fairly? Same questions and option names; its documentation recommends a description per option, which may raise its score. — Why 1.7B? To prove the path on one 12 GB card. Larger Gemma and Phi donors need more time or a bigger GPU; Fuuga offloads GPU memory to RAM and SSD. — Does the text model beat OpenAI or Anthropic? It is compared with itself; that comparison runs in each pilot, on data no hosted model has seen.
