Bars are proportional to the percentages. The engineering share is the argument: a product company funding its own builders rather than a services firm buying a sales team. Detail — Engineering (£1.375m): four engineers from close and two ML engineers from month seven — about eleven engineer-years at a fully loaded £110k, plus contractors and tooling. London and remote, at £110k — a London median base of about £85k times the usual 1.25–1.4 on-cost. — Sales and marketing (£450k): a head of sales from month seven and, from year two, a developer-relations lead who also runs partner enablement. — GPU compute (£300k): our own base models, such as the Nordic-language release. Customer training runs on the customer's own hardware or cloud. — Operations, legal and compliance (£250k): security review, the EU AI Act audit pack, trademark and contracts. — Reserve (£125k). The raise — £2.5m sits at the top of the usual London seed range, which the AI premium supports: UK median seed deals were about £560k in 2024, and AI deals carry a markedly higher median at every stage.
