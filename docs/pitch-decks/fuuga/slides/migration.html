Adoption path

The bill is the business case. Move a workload only when the numbers say so.

01

Connect, do not rewrite. Fuuga speaks both the OpenAI and the Anthropic interface, and serves them together by default. An application built on either is pointed at a new address.

02

Run both, route by rule. Sensitive or routine requests go to the in-house model, the rest to the existing subscription, within a budget.

03

Learn from the incumbent. Fuuga trains the in-house model on the hosted model's answers to the company's own prompts.

04

Switch when the numbers say so. A typed judge compares old against new on the company's own work, not a benchmark. When it passes, the workload moves and the subscription shrinks.

Why a company bothers

  • Every switch is justified by a measured comparison, not a promise
  • The workloads compliance currently blocks become possible
  • A per-request bill becomes a fixed, predictable cost
  • No vendor can retire the model underneath a live product
  • The weights leave as GGUF, safetensors or ONNX — it runs anywhere, including without us
