Code is the cheap part now. What a fork does not inherit is the business: the rate of execution, the customer's record, and somebody accountable. Detail — Rate: one engineer directing AI agents ships new attention variants, GRPO and dynamic quantisation within weeks of the papers, under test. — One stack: the next problem is a new head, not a rewrite, under the same audit trail. — Record: only we hold a customer's calibration history, drift record and training provenance. — Accountability: buyers pay for supported builds, current evidence and a named party when the model is wrong. Answers — Why give the code away? Releasing first puts the work on public record as prior art. — Won't a large vendor copy it? A vendor's own stack moves at a vendor's pace. — Can our own engineers do it with AI? They can start; finishing means owning the maintenance, the evidence and the accountability.
