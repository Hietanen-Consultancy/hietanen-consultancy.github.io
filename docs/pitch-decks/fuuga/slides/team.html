Lead engineer at Basware on purchase-to-pay at global scale. Maintainer of SQLProvider and LINQKit, published .NET author, and the author of Fuuga. — Method: one engineer directing AI coding agents, every module under unit and property tests, the whole source public, funded by consultancy income rather than a round. It is the method the product sells, run on ourselves. — Capacity: one engineer is assigned full time today. A network of senior engineers we have shipped with joins an engagement on contract when the work needs more hands, so delivery is not capped at one person; names on request, not on the slide. — Hires: engineers, a data lead, a seller for regulated enterprise and a compliance lead, each when signed revenue pays for them; a round would bring them forward, with a developer-relations lead for partner enablement. — Key-person risk: the code is public domain and under test, every model's recipe is written down and rerunnable, and the first hires — or engineers from the network — can run that loop without the founder.
