Detail — We run it at £100k a year: one engineer at £110k runs about three engines, about 60% margin; our estimate, set by the first contracts. — Half of customers have us run it: the split most worth testing in the first ten sales. — Continuity at £60k renews as teams turn over; partners at £30k, five then twenty. — Sellers from month seven, five closes a year each; engineers at £110k fully loaded (a London median of about £85k times 1.25–1.4). — Margins: consulting 45%, running it 60%, continuity and partners 80%; training compute is the customer's. Answers — Residency? In-region hosting answers most buyers; the market is the ones it does not. — Microsoft adds training? Fuuga implements Microsoft.Extensions.AI, so their progress widens the funnel. — Key person? Public, tested code makes the work transferable. — Sales cycle? Two to three months for a vendor, six for a regulated buyer. — Training on a hosted model's output? Decision engines train on the customer's own outcomes; counsel's view on distillation is in the data room. — GPU cost volatility? Consumer-GPU fine-tuning and CPU serving are design goals.
