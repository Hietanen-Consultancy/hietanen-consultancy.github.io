This is the plan we run whether or not we raise: never loss-making, about two years slower. The round accelerates it; it does not rescue it. Detail — Rule: a hire waits until signed recurring revenue covers the payroll about 1.2 times; managed contracts are invoiced a year ahead. — Now: consulting covers the company's running costs of about £40k a year, and part of the week goes to pilots on customers' recorded outcomes. — £200k signed pays the first engineer; about £500k adds a data lead and a commission-led seller; about £800k adds a compliance lead and two engineers, and the founder stops consulting. — At twenty-two customers, revenue is about £2.0m against £1.03–1.08m of cost, assuming AI-assisted operation of about six engines per engineer. — Funding without equity: UK R&D tax relief, contracts paid in advance, paid pilots. Answers — Why raise at all? Speed, partners at scale, and a seller in year one rather than year three.
