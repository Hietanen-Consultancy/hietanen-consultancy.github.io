Detail — One toolchain covers the whole life of a model: preparing data, training, fine-tuning, shrinking, testing and running it. There is no separate research stack, no second team and no hand-over problem. — It installs like any other business software component, on the company's own servers or cloud, and starts from the best open models: Llama, Phi, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma and DeepSeek. — Applications already built on OpenAI or Anthropic connect without being rewritten, and the finished weights export to the standard formats, so nothing is trapped here. — .NET is the platform these companies' core systems already run on, staffed by engineers they already employ. "No Python" means no second, incompatible toolchain owned by a specialist. — The terminal panel is the shortest path for a technical reader who wants to check the repository.
