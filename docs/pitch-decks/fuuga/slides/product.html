The product

Your own AI model, built and maintained by the engineers you already have.

Fuuga is the complete toolchain for making a language model, or retraining an existing one — preparing data, training, fine-tuning, shrinking, testing and running it — delivered on .NET, the platform most banks, insurers and enterprises already run on. No separate research stack, no second team, no hand-over problem.

  • Installs like any other business software component, on the company's own servers or cloud
  • Starts from the best open models (Llama, Phi, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, DeepSeek) and teaches them the company's data
  • Runs on ordinary GPUs or plain CPU servers; no data leaves the building
  • Speaks both the OpenAI and the Anthropic interface, so applications built on either connect without being rewritten — and the finished weights export to the standard formats, so nothing is trapped here

for the technical reader — the common path

> fuuga scaffold finetune
> fuuga transfer --recipe my.json

> fuuga export gguf --tokenizer tok/
