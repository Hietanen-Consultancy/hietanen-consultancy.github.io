Your own AI model, built and maintained by the engineers you already have.

The complete toolchain to make a language model, or retrain an existing one — on .NET, the platform banks and insurers already run.

  • Starts from open models you know — Llama, Qwen, Gemma, Mistral, Phi, DeepSeek, Kimi — and teaches them your data
  • Runs on ordinary GPUs or CPU servers you already own
  • Speaks the OpenAI and Anthropic interfaces; nothing is rewritten

Detail — One toolchain covers the whole life of a model: preparing data, training, fine-tuning, shrinking, testing and running it. There is no separate research stack, no second team and no hand-over problem. — It installs like any other business software component, on the company's own servers or cloud, and starts from open models: Llama 3, Qwen 2 and 3, Gemma 3 and 4, Mistral, Phi-3 and Phi-4, DeepSeek-V3 and Kimi K2, with GLM and Kimi K3 experimental. Any of them can be read straight from a GGUF file, and ModernBERT is the starting point for typed decisions. — Applications already built on OpenAI or Anthropic connect without being rewritten, and the finished weights export to the standard formats, so nothing is trapped here. — .NET is the platform these companies' core systems already run on, staffed by engineers they already employ. "No Python" means no second, incompatible toolchain owned by a specialist. — The terminal panel is the shortest path for a technical reader who wants to check the repository.
