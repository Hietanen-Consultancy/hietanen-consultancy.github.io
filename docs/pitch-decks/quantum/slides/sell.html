What we sell, and who with

We sell the plan. Our partners sell the fleet.

What we sell

Licence — embedded planning SDK. The planner ships inside the products that already fly fleets.

Usage — Swarm Planning API. Fleet and constraints in; routes, assignments and MAVLink missions out. Billed per plan and per aircraft.

Projects — integration and pilots. Twelve weeks to a planner in production, converting to a licence or to usage.

What the partner gets

A capability sold with the fleet. Coordinated operations goes in the package at their margin, per aircraft per month — closing deals their competitors cannot describe.

Evidence for the flight manual. The ratio a regulator grants is capped by the automation the manufacturer documents. Only they can make that claim; we make it cheap to support.

An upsell, not a replacement cycle. MAVLink and QGroundControl export, so fleets already delivered can buy coordinated planning without changing an aircraft.

The chain. A wind farm, utility, grower or fire service pays a drone service operator, who buys aircraft and software from our partners, who license the planner from us. Every aircraft sold is recurring revenue for both of us. The business works at one pilot per aircraft; the higher ratio is upside the partner claims.
