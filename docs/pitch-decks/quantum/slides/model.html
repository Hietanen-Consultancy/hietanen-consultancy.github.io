Business model

Open library. Licences from vendors, usage from fleets, no aircraft on the balance sheet.

01 · Licence

Embedded planning SDK

For vendors and integrators shipping fleet software: signed builds, support, an SLA, and the planning evidence their customers need in an authorisation file. [£__k] a year.

02 · Usage

Swarm Planning API

Hosted: fleet, tasks and constraints in; routes, assignments and MAVLink missions out. Billed per plan and per aircraft. The line that scales as their fleets do.

03 · Projects

Integration and pilots

Fixed scope with a vendor or an operator: their constraints, a planner in production in twelve weeks. Converts to a licence or to usage.

04 · Channel

Aircraft and platform partners

Coordinated operations bundled into a fleet package, billed per aircraft per month at the partner's margin. Both sides earn from the same order.

The library stays public domain so any developer can start. Revenue comes when a plan has to be right, on time, on a real fleet — and it arrives through the companies already selling to that fleet. QPU time is resold at cost plus a margin alongside, never as a growth line.
