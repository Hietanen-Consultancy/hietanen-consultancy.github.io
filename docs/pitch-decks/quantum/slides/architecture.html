How it works

Three layers. Developers touch only the top one.

Layer 1

Builders — the business vocabulary

TSP, scheduling, assignment, knapsack, portfolio, network flow; fraud, churn, workforce. Written by .NET developers or by their agents.

Layer 2

Solvers — the algorithms

QAOA for optimisation, VQE and ADAPT-VQE for chemistry, Grover, QFT-based arithmetic and phase estimation.

Layer 3

Backends — one interface, every machine

Local simulator, gate-based QPUs through Azure Quantum and Braket, D-Wave annealers, a topological simulator. Circuits arrive as OpenQASM from Qiskit and leave as OpenQASM, QIR or a MAVLink mission.

Quantum-first, honestly

A quantum solver never silently hands back a classical answer: if the hardware cannot solve it, the caller gets an error, not a disguise. HybridSolver routes to a classical baseline only when asked.
