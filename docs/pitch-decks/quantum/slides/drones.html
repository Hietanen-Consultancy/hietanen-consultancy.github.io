Airframe, wind, GPS error and radio loss need the real aircraft. — The air bridge plans corridors, with drones as units of flow on them; the classical planner is the oracle the quantum one has to beat. Its customers are aerial-services contractors for fire and rescue. — Choreography runs four drones on sixteen qubits, and a hundred drones on the same sixteen qubits, four at a time.
