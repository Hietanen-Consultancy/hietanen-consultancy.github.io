The product

One NuGet package. Business problem in, quantum-computed answer out.

A .NET developer — or the agent working alongside them — describes routing, scheduling, assignment or portfolio problems in ordinary F# or C# and solves them on a simulator or on real hardware: the same code, every backend.

  • On NuGet; F# computation expressions and a C# fluent API
  • Seven optimisation builders, business builders for scheduling, fraud and churn, and quantum ML
  • Backends: local simulator, Azure Quantum (IonQ, Rigetti, Quantinuum, Atom Computing), D-Wave, AWS Braket, topological simulator
  • OpenQASM 1.0–3.0 in and out, QIR emission, MAVLink missions; error mitigation and qubit routing
