A scenario built from the drivers on the next slide, not a forecast with evidence behind it yet. Licences come first because a vendor is reachable now; usage becomes the largest software line in year three because it grows with their customers' fleets rather than with our sales team. How the lines are built — Recurring lines count customers signed evenly through the year: revenue is the average of the opening and closing count times the price. — Licences at £40k: one, five and nine vendors at year end — £0.01m, £0.12m and £0.28m; year one counts only the first licence, signed around month nine. — Usage at £6k a month: two, five and sixteen operators at year end — £0.02m, £0.25m and £0.76m; year one counts only the last weeks, once the API beta is live. — Projects at £150k: about three, five and six a year. Neither line depends on quantum advantage arriving on schedule.
